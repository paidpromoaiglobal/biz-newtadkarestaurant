Our Story

Made with Passion

Tadka – Aromas of North is a beloved North Indian restaurant nestled in the heart of Tellapur, Hyderabad, at Level Up Drive In. We bring the rich, bold, and aromatic culinary traditions of North India to your plate — from smoky tandoor-kissed kebabs to slow-cooked gravies that warm the soul. With a stellar rating of 4.8 out of 5, we have quickly become a go-to destination for food lovers seeking genuine flavour at honest prices.

Whether you are dining in for a hearty meal or ordering from the comfort of your home, Tadka ensures every dish is prepared with care, quality ingredients, and a deep respect for authentic recipes. Our menu features crowd favourites like Achari Chicken Tikka, Afghani Chicken, Butter Chicken, Tangdi Kabab, and Kadhai Chicken — each dish a testament to the vibrant aromas of the North. Open every day from noon to midnight, we are always ready to serve you an unforgettable meal.
